Document Validation Service

Healthtech · 2026 · Designed and built it; tech lead of the team that runs it
AWS LambdaSQSAPI GatewayPostgreSQLDynamoDBLLMs

Problem

Insurers and hospitals send claim forms and authorization documents that must be checked: document type, signature, stamp, consistency between fields. The rules change by country, document type, and contract. The service replaced a legacy validation flow.

What I built

An event-driven serverless service. An LLM classifies each document and extracts its fields, then a rules engine configurable per country, document type, and contract validates them. To switch over safely, I ran the legacy system and the new service on the same real production cases and compared the results case by case.

03

Medical Pre-Audit with AI

Healthtech · 2026 · Author of the RFC, tech lead of the project
ClaudeGeminiFallbackPrompt versioningFew-shotSSO

Problem

Auditing a hospital bill means reading the clinical record and the invoice side by side. The business goal is to bring a 2–4 hour audit under 2 hours.

What I designed

A system that cross-checks the clinical record against the invoice and raises alerts in six categories: under- and over-billing, duplicates, charges outside the stay, clinical inference, and clinical guidelines. Results are cached by SHA-256 hash, prompts are versioned in the database, token cost is tracked per request from day one, and structured feedback from auditors feeds curated few-shot examples.

fig. 2 · two providers in parallel, automatic fallback

A decision I'm glad I made

I dropped a planned OCR layer. It added indirection without adding value, and one more thing to break.

04

Running LLMs like production infrastructure

Healthtech · 2026 · Incident lead and owner of LLM cost tracking
Incident responsePostmortemsLLM FinOps

The incident

Our main LLM provider stopped responding from the production VPC. Within hours I moved the document processors to another provider with a rollback path ready, then stabilized and migrated back to a newer model. I wrote the postmortem and a side-by-side evidence report of both providers in production.

The cost ledger

I built a per-request ledger of LLM usage and cost, with pricing that has effective dates, and a tool that computes the real cost of extraction (LLM plus Lambda) per processor and environment. It surfaced a pricing error that had been understating what we really paid.
